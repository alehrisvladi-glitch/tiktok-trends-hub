Master Instagram Reels with expert tips. Learn what content goes viral and how to maximize reach.

Reels Growth Tips

  • Hook in 3 Seconds: Grab attention immediately or viewers scroll
  • Use Trending Audio: Reels with trending sounds get more reach
  • Post Consistently: Daily posting signals activity to algorithm
  • Engage with Comments: Reply to boost engagement metrics
  • Optimal Length: 15-30 seconds performs best for most niches

Best Posting Times

Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11 AM and 7-9 PM typically perform well. Test different times for your specific audience.
