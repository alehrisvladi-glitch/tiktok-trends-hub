Crack the Algorithm Code

Learn how TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube decide what content to promote.

Algorithm Factors

  • Watch Time: Total time viewers watch your videos
  • Retention Rate: Percentage of video watched
  • Engagement: Likes, comments, shares, and saves
  • Consistency: Regular posting signals active creator

Optimization Tips

Create hooks that grab attention immediately. Keep videos concise. Encourage engagement with CTAs. Post when your audience is most active.
