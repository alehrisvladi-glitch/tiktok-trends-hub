How Social Media Algorithms Work

Crack the code of TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube algorithms. Optimize for maximum reach.

Key Algorithm Factors

  • Watch Time: Total time viewers spend watching your content
  • Retention Rate: Percentage of video watched (aim for 70%+)
  • Engagement Rate: Likes, comments, shares relative to views
  • Posting Consistency: Regular uploads signal active creator
  • Niche Relevance: Content consistency helps algorithm categorize you

Platform Differences

TikTok prioritizes watch time and completion rate. Instagram values saves and shares. YouTube focuses on watch time and session time.
